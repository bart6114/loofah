# Loofah Notes

**Subtitle** Voice notes for your Mac

**Keywords** voice recorder,meeting notes,offline notes,voice memo,audio capture,meeting recorder

**Support URL** https://loofah.io/help/

**Privacy Policy URL** https://loofah.io/privacy-policy/

## Description

Capture a thought, conversation, or meeting on your iPhone and pick it up in Loofah on your Mac.

Tap Record and type notes while the microphone is running. For a text-only capture, tap New note. You can give either a title and edit the title and notes until the Mac starts importing it. You can do both offline. Recording continues when you switch apps or lock your iPhone. You can check the timer and stop from the Live Activity. Loofah uses your Apple Account’s iCloud Drive to send the capture to the Mac you designate. The iPhone keeps it until the Mac saves it in your vault.

Open the capture alongside your other notes on your Mac. Loofah transcribes recordings on your Mac.

Handoff requires iCloud Drive and the Loofah Mac app signed into the same Apple Account. Download Loofah for Mac at loofah.io.

## App Review walkthrough

1. On iPhone, open Loofah and tap **New note**. Enter a title and a sentence, then tap **Done**. Reopen the note under **Waiting for sync** to edit it before the Mac imports it.
2. Tap **Record**, grant microphone access, speak a few sentences, and type a note. Go to the Home Screen or lock your iPhone while recording. Expand the Dynamic Island or use the Lock Screen Live Activity to stop and save.
3. For the handoff, install the current Loofah release for Mac from loofah.io. Enable iCloud Drive on both devices with the same Apple Account. On your Mac, open **Settings → Storage**, check the vault location, and enable **Receive iPhone captures on this Mac**.
4. Open the captures in the Mac vault. The phone removes each item after the Mac confirms it was saved. With a Mac speech model configured, the recording is transcribed locally.
5. For the privacy policy, tap the information button beside **Waiting for sync**, then tap **Privacy policy**.

Loofah requires no separate login or purchase. iCloud handles the handoff, and the iPhone app can record and write offline without a Mac present.


## App Privacy

Use [Loofah’s privacy policy](https://loofah.io/privacy-policy/) for the Privacy Policy URL. The iPhone app uses Apple iCloud Drive for the handoff. Loofah has no capture backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, or iPhone AI provider integrations. Under Apple’s definition of collection, this supports **Data Not Collected** for the iPhone app. Review the existing App Store Connect answers against the shipping build before publishing them.
