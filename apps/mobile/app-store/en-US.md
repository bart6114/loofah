# Loofah Notes

Subtitle: Private notes and transcripts

Support URL: https://loofah.io/help/

Privacy Policy URL: https://loofah.io/privacy-policy/

## Description

Keep notes and recordings together on your iPhone. Loofah lets you write while you record, then turns the microphone audio into a transcript on your device.

- Record audio with a clear recording indicator and controls you can reach from the Lock Screen.
- Take notes during a recording and edit them afterward.
- Make Loofah your own with eight design themes. Default follows your iPhone’s light or dark appearance; literary, colorful, and dark themes bring their own palettes and typography. Find them in Settings → Appearance.
- Import an audio file to transcribe an existing conversation.
- Download a speech model once, then transcribe on your iPhone without sending the recording to a transcription service.
- Keep your notes in a private local vault, or connect an iCloud Drive vault to sync with Loofah on Mac.
- Optionally generate summaries using an AI provider you configure. Settings explains the data sent and names the provider and server. Sharing starts only after you give permission and is handled under that provider's terms.

Loofah is free and does not require an account or subscription. You decide what to record and whether to connect iCloud Drive or an AI provider.

## App Review notes

No account is needed. To test note editing, tap the plus button and create a note. To test recording, tap Record and allow microphone access; the active recording appears in the app and as a Live Activity. Transcription requires a model download from Settings → Transcription, so allow the download to finish before testing it. Audio import is available from the home screen. iCloud Drive sync and AI summaries are optional; the core note, recording, and transcription flows work without either service.

AI privacy review: open Settings → Summaries and select a provider. The disclosure identifies the recipient and server, explains the note, transcript, metadata, and summary data sent, and links to the privacy policy. Permission is initially unchecked, including after an upgrade with existing provider settings. Saving settings without checking permission does not enable AI sharing. Explicit permission enables manual summaries and summaries after transcription, plus generated titles. Clear permission and save, or select Off and save, to stop future requests. Changing provider, server, or model clears permission. Recording and transcription remain on-device and available without AI consent.
