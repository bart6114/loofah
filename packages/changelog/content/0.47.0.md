## Receive iPhone captures on your Mac

In Loofah for Mac, open **Settings → Storage** and enable **Receive iPhone captures on this Mac**. Turn on iCloud Drive on both devices using the same Apple Account.

When you finish a note or stop a recording in the Loofah iPhone beta, it appears in your Mac vault. You can capture offline, and the iPhone keeps each item until the Mac confirms it’s saved. If you have a speech model configured, your Mac transcribes recordings locally.
