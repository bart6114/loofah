## New

- Generating or regenerating a summary now asks your AI provider for a confidence score for each suggested tag. Tags scoring above 85% are attached automatically; tags at 85% or below stay as suggestions for you to accept or dismiss.
- **Auto-apply tags with high confidence** is on by default in **Settings → Summaries**. Turn it off to review every suggestion manually. Changing the setting applies to future summaries and leaves existing tags and suggestions in place.

## Changes

- Regenerating a summary preserves attached tags and dismissed suggestions. Confidence scores are the model's estimates of relevance, not calibrated probabilities.
- The summary customization guide now explains tag confidence, automatic application, and manual review.
