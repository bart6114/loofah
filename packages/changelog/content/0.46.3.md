## Dictation

When transcription finishes, Loofah sends **Command+V** on macOS or **Ctrl+V** on Windows to the app with keyboard focus. This lets you dictate into more places, including embedded terminals and custom editors. Loofah restores your clipboard after the paste and keeps anything you copied in the meantime.

On macOS, Loofah refreshes the dictation shortcut when you switch apps after granting Accessibility. This fixes a case where holding **Ctrl+Option+Space** could type spaces while you dictated.

If the words don’t appear, use **Copy Last Dictation** in the menu bar or tray menu.
