## New

- Summaries now ask the AI to include task checkboxes for outstanding actions
  clearly assigned to you or committed to by you. Other people's actions stay
  ordinary summary bullets, and unclear ownership does not create a personal task.

## Fixes

- Tasks from generated summaries appear in your task list even when you never
  open the summary. Regenerating a summary preserves completion, due dates, and
  other saved details for matching tasks.
- Summaries are no longer cut off by automatic character or section limits. The
  AI is guided to stay concise while preserving decisions and explicit actions.
- Imported audio no longer assumes that its first audio channel belongs to you
  when identifying personal action items.
- Ordinary bullets mixed with task checkboxes stay ordinary bullets, including
  in nested lists.
