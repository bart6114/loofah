## New

- Summarizing a note now also asks your selected AI provider to suggest up to three relevant tags. Suggestions favor tags already in your vault, with new tags proposed when useful.
- You choose which suggestions to accept or dismiss. Attached tags stay in place, and dismissed tags are excluded from future suggestions for that note.

## Changes

- Tag suggestions are generated alongside summaries. Editing notes, importing recordings, and transcribing no longer calculate tags separately, and hashtags in note text no longer attach tags automatically.
- The automatic tag application setting has been removed. Existing attached tags and dismissals are preserved; older pending suggestions are cleared. Generate a summary to get fresh suggestions.
