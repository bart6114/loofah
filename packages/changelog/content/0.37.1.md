## New

- Whisper models can now show text while you record. Select a downloaded model
  in Settings > Transcription and choose While recording. Text arrives in speech
  chunks, with support for the selected model's languages and your dictionary
  terms.
- Choose While recording or After recording under Transcription timing. Loofah
  remembers your choice and explains when a model or language selection requires
  transcription after recording.
- Set meeting languages in Transcription and the language for generated text in
  Summaries. Changing the summary language no longer changes your meeting's
  transcription languages.
- Settings now groups recording controls and microphone reminders under
  Recording, gives Storage its own page, and renames Intelligence to Summaries.
  Summary setup offers separate choices for a ChatGPT subscription, an API key,
  or a local AI app.
- Onboarding now distinguishes downloaded, downloading, and unconfigured
  transcription models, and shows whether a summary service has been selected.
  The default microphone-reminder delay is now five seconds.

## Fixes

- Live transcription preserves the order of audio captured during startup or
  reconnection, helping avoid missing or garbled opening words.
- Parakeet live transcription better recognizes quiet microphone input,
  including low-volume headset speech.
- Live transcripts correctly attribute your microphone speech to you.
- The summary-prompt editor preserves unsaved drafts while navigating settings
  and asks before discarding them. Summary connection checks also show when a
  retry is in progress and offer clearer setup errors.
- Korean summaries use the same general writing instructions as other
  languages, without the previous Korean-specific sentence-ending rules.
