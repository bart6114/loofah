## New

- Choose **Nemotron 3.5 ASR (Multilingual)** in transcription settings for live recording and imported audio in 28 languages, including Dutch. The model downloads once and transcribes on your Mac.
- Nemotron uses your selected meeting languages as guidance, including after pauses. Selecting multiple languages gives the model a preference; occasional language-detection mistakes are still possible.

## Changed

- The audio player expands across the available space in the note header, with a taller, clearer waveform. It keeps its minimum width before switching to the compact play button.
- **CLI:** `loof transcribe` supports the configured Nemotron model and uses the meeting languages saved in your vault settings.

## Fixes

- If Nemotron live transcription fails, recording continues and transcription is retried after you stop.
