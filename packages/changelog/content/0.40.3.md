## Fixes

- Microphone reminders keep their 30-second countdown when you focus Loofah or
  navigate through notification settings. Hovering pauses the countdown, including
  when the reminder appears beneath your pointer. Closing the reminder or choosing
  an action still dismisses it immediately.
- Clicking a microphone reminder while a recording is already starting or running
  opens that recording's note without starting another recording or changing its
  meeting-app tracking.
- Note and summary edits are preserved while content refreshes or you switch
  notes. Failed saves keep your draft available within the app and show an error
  instead of allowing older content to replace your edits.
- Pressing Enter in a note title creates a body paragraph. Backspace at the start
  of the body and Delete at the end of the title no longer accidentally join the
  two.
