## Fixes

- Recording reminders now appear as soon as Loofah detects microphone use in a
  supported meeting app. The **Show reminder after** setting has been removed.
- Reminders stay visible for 30 seconds before dismissing automatically. Hovering
  over a reminder pauses the countdown so you have time to choose **Start recording**.
