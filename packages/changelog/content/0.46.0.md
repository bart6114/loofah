## Dictation

Type into other apps with your voice. Set up **Dictation** in **Settings → App**, then hold **Ctrl+Option+Space** on macOS or **Ctrl+Alt+Space** on Windows, speak, and release to insert your words. You can also choose press-to-start/stop mode.

Dictation uses your on-device transcription model and language settings. It does not create a meeting note. If insertion fails, use **Copy text**; if transcription fails, **Retry** reuses the recording and offers the result to copy.

## Windows downloads

Windows installers and CLI downloads now carry a publisher signature on both x64 and ARM64.
