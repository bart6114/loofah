---

## New

- Choose Whisper in Settings > Transcription on Apple Silicon Macs. Available
  models include multilingual Large V3 and Large Turbo, plus Base and Small in
  multilingual and English-only versions. Download a model, then select it to
  transcribe recordings and imported audio on your Mac after recording ends.
- Whisper uses terms from Settings > Dictionary as transcription hints, helping
  it recognize names, acronyms, and technical vocabulary.

## Fixes

- Transcription language warnings now check your main language as well as
  additional spoken languages. They show the selected model's name and update
  when you change your language settings.
