## Fixes

- Large recordings no longer hit the local Whisper upload size limit or time out while transcription and speaker detection are still working.
- Whisper and Parakeet batch transcription use less memory when preparing and processing long recordings. Speaker labels still cover the whole recording.
- Failed transcriptions with no words now show an error and a retry action instead of a blank Transcript tab.
- Upload audio and Upload transcript stay available in notes that already contain text, as long as the note has no recording or transcript and no recording is in progress.
