## Fixes

- Imported stereo recordings, including files from iPhone Voice Memos, are now
  transcribed as one conversation. Speech captured in both channels no longer
  appears twice under different speakers, and genuine repetitions are preserved.
- Re-transcribing imported audio keeps its imported-audio handling after app
  restarts or transcript deletion. Interrupted imports now ask you to import the
  file again before transcribing or recording.
- CLI: `loof import` and `loof transcribe` use the same imported-audio handling as
  the desktop app, including when replacing an existing meeting's audio or
  supplying a recording date.
