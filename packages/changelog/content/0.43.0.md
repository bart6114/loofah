## New

- Choose a design theme in Settings → General. Fieldnotes, Signal, Forma, Margin, and Workshop offer distinct light styles; Midnight and Ember offer dark styles. Default keeps neutral light and dark colors, the original Loofah logo, and your light, dark, or system appearance setting.

## Changed

- Notes, summaries, and transcripts use larger text, a centered reading column, and more consistent spacing. Theme-specific fonts and shapes carry through the editor, sidebar, and controls.
- Sidebar navigation, settings, speaker labels, timestamps, and playback controls have clearer contrast. The theme picker previews each style before you select it.
