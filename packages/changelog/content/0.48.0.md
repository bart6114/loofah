## Meeting links on Mac

Click a Loofah meeting link to open the matching note in your current Mac vault.

## iPhone captures

After import, Loofah for Mac transcribes recordings on-device. If you’ve configured a summary model, it also summarizes eligible written notes and recordings automatically. A configured remote summary provider receives the session context needed to generate the summary.

Untitled iPhone captures now appear in the sidebar with a preview of their notes when available. Closing a note tab also preserves recordings and imported captures that have no title or note text.
