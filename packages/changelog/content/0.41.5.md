## New

- About Loofah now has a Diagnostics tab with a copy button for sharing app, device, transcription, and Intelligence details when reporting an issue. The report separates saved settings, configuration resolved from those settings, and current activity. It excludes credentials and note, transcript, and recording content.
- Local transcription models show their readable names, including Whisper, alongside their internal identifiers. The report also shows model availability, language support, and fallback reasons.
