## Personal to-dos

The default summary adds a personal to-do when the transcript shows you explicitly taking it on. If someone else owns the work, you’ll see a to-do only for a follow-up you agreed to handle. Other people’s actions stay in the summary.

## iPhone beta

If a call or another audio interruption pauses a recording, Loofah can continue the same capture when iOS makes the microphone available again. If the recording stays paused, tap **Resume** to continue or **Done** to keep what you’ve recorded. Audio from before and after the pause reaches your Mac in one recording.
