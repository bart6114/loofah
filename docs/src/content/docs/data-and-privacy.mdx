Loofah has no account or cloud backend. Your vault is the source of truth for notes, transcripts, recordings, attachments, and settings. Recording happens on your device without adding a bot to a call. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) for the full disclosure.

## Files in the vault

The app stores notes, transcripts, attachments, settings, recordings, and people as files in the vault. On Mac, the default vault is in the app's data directory. You can change its location under **Settings → Storage**. On iPhone, Loofah stores drafts and finished captures in private app storage.

To use the same vault on another Mac, move it into a supported local sync folder. The iPhone app keeps drafts locally and sends finished captures through an app-private inbox in your iCloud account. It connects to iCloud for handoff. The iPhone removes a capture once the designated Mac confirms that it saved the capture in its vault. See [Sync your notes](/syncing).

You can inspect and edit the vault with other tools. The app watches for external changes and refreshes its in-memory index. If the app is about to overwrite a version it did not write, it first moves that version into the vault's `.trash/` folder.

Downloaded transcription and speaker-detection models are also local, but they are managed separately from meeting files.

## Remote Intelligence on Mac

Transcription and speaker detection run locally on your Mac. If you enable a remote Intelligence provider, the app sends the prompt and session context needed for summaries and titles. For an iPhone capture, Loofah asks on the Mac before sharing content with an external provider. The permission prompt shows the recipient and the data involved. iCloud or another sync service can also receive vault files if you choose to connect it.

API keys are stored in the macOS login Keychain. Review a provider's retention and training policy before using it with sensitive meetings. Loofah is free, but a provider you choose may charge separately.

For local summaries on Mac, use LM Studio or Ollama. See [Use the app offline](/offline).

## Audio retention

Your Mac keeps vault recordings until you delete them. Transcription keeps the vault recording, including when you transcribe through the CLI.

On Mac, you can explicitly delete a recording without removing its note, summary, or transcript. To find a recording, open the meeting's **•••** menu and choose **Show in Finder**.

## Telemetry

Loofah does not send usage analytics or telemetry.

:::caution
Recording-consent rules vary by location. Make sure you have permission before recording.
:::
