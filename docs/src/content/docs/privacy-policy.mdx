## Loofah for iPhone

Loofah uses your microphone when you start a recording. You can record and write notes offline. Drafts stay in private app storage on your iPhone. While you record, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island can show the recording status and elapsed time.

Loofah uses Apple’s iCloud Drive to send finished captures to the Mac you designate. This includes recordings Loofah recovers and finishes when you reopen the app. Each capture includes the note, timestamps, and any recording. The apps use a private inbox under your Apple Account. They also sync a receiver identifier and delivery confirmations. Apple handles the iCloud transfer and storage. Loofah has no account service or server that receives your captures. [Apple Account and iCloud privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-account/)

Loofah keeps the finished capture on your iPhone until your Mac confirms it saved the capture. The apps then clear the capture from the phone and iCloud inbox. You can stop future handoff by turning off iCloud access for Loofah in iPhone Settings. To remove a draft or pending capture from your phone, delete the app and its local data. This removes local copies of captures that may still be waiting for your Mac. Use Apple’s iCloud storage controls for data already in iCloud.

The iPhone app sends captures through iCloud. It has no AI provider integrations, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Loofah does not sell your data.

## Loofah for Mac

Your Mac stores notes, recordings, transcripts, summaries, and settings as files in your vault. Your Mac transcribes audio and detects speakers on the device. A model download host may see connection information such as your IP address.

If you configure a remote Intelligence provider, the Mac sends the prompt and relevant session context for summaries or titles. This includes automatic summaries for notes and recordings imported from your iPhone. Summary requests can include notes, transcript text, speaker names, and session details. Title requests can include the generated summary. Requests use the provider you chose, including any routing it performs. The Mac stores provider API keys in the macOS login Keychain.

Turn off your summary model to stop automatic summaries. Contact the provider about retention or deletion of data you already sent. If you put the vault in a synced folder, the sync service handles those copies under its own policy.

Your vault keeps files until you delete them. You can remove a recording or note in Loofah. Manage any copies in your backups or sync services separately. Loofah does not receive your vault files in normal use.
