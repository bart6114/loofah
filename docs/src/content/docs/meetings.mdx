---
title: "Record meetings without a bot on Mac"
description: "Record microphone and system audio on your Mac, transcribe meetings locally, and keep your own notes without adding a bot to the call."
---

Loofah records both sides of a call from your Mac and transcribes on-device. There is no meeting bot to invite and no account to create. Download the free Apple Silicon app and prepare a speech model with the [first-meeting guide](/quickstart).

## Start recording

Create a **New Note**, or open an existing empty note, then click **Record** in the top-right corner.

Loofah captures your microphone and system audio on your Mac. It does not join the call as a participant.

:::caution
Make sure you have permission to record. Consent rules vary by location.
:::

## While the meeting is running

Write your own notes in **Memos**. Those notes are included as context if you generate a summary later.

The **Transcript** view shows text during the meeting when the active model and language support live transcription. Otherwise, transcription runs after you stop recording.

Turn on **Show floating bar** in **Settings → App → Meetings** if you want compact controls while the main window is out of the way.

## Finish or resume

Click **Stop listening**. The app finishes any pending transcription and generates a summary when an Intelligence model is configured and the transcript is long enough.

You can resume the same note from the recording control if the conversation continues.

## Start from an existing file

On an empty note, open the **•••** menu and choose **Upload audio** or **Upload transcript**. See [Import audio or a transcript](/import-recordings) for the supported formats.

You can also import from the terminal: `loof import recording.m4a --transcribe` creates the meeting and transcribes it without opening the app. See the [CLI reference](/reference/cli).

For microphone reminders and automatic stopping, see [Automate meeting capture](/automatic-capture).

## Keep the meeting useful

[Name detected speakers](/ai-setup#speaker-detection) after recording, [choose a summary template](/customize-summaries), or [export the session as Markdown](/notes#export-a-session). Your notes and summaries stay in your local vault; [recordings are kept indefinitely](/data-and-privacy#audio-retention).
